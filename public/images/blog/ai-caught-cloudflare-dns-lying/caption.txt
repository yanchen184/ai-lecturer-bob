切 DNS 最大的坑不是設錯，是「設對了但驗證工具騙你」。

時間軸：
- 00:00 在 Porkbun 把 NS 改成 Cloudflare
- 00:02 / 00:10 / 00:20 / 00:28 連跑四次 dig @1.1.1.1，都說還是 Porkbun
- 我準備回 Porkbun 後台檢查是不是哪裡按錯

這時 AI 說：「先別動。1.1.1.1 可能 stale cache，我同時打 8.8.8.8 / 9.9.9.9 / 208.67.222.222 對驗。」

結果三家都回新 NS。

只有 Cloudflare 自己的 1.1.1.1 還抱著舊紀錄。原因是 1.1.1.1 為了「全球最快 resolver」用激進 cache 策略 — 對一般使用者是優點，對在切 DNS 的工程師是惡夢。

人類傾向「相信工具」，AI 傾向「工具是另一個變數」。這個思考方式的差異救了我至少 1 小時。

A small DNS debugging story that taught me what AI engineers actually do better than humans.

I switched my nameservers from Porkbun to Cloudflare. Then I ran `dig @1.1.1.1 yanchen.app NS` and it kept returning the old NS for 30 minutes. I was about to revert.

Claude Code (running my migration) suggested: "Try 8.8.8.8 and 9.9.9.9 simultaneously — 1.1.1.1 might have stale cache."

Three resolvers agreed on the new NS. Only 1.1.1.1 was lying.

The insight isn't about DNS. It's about a mindset:
- Humans: trust the tool, doubt your own work
- AI: treat the tool as another variable, cross-verify

AI doesn't have the "I already did this, I don't want to verify again" emotion. It'll run the same dig four times in parallel without complaint. Humans won't.

This is exactly the kind of infra task where AI shines: noisy systems with caching layers, propagation delays, and ambiguous failure modes.
